What is a Priority Queue?

Unlike a standard Queue (FIFO - First In, First Out), a Priority Queue assigns a priority to each element. Elements with higher priorities are served before elements with lower priorities, regardless of when they arrived.

Real-World Analogy

Emergency room triage: Patients with life-threatening injuries are treated first, even if they arrived after someone with a minor cold.

Core Operations

Insert (Enqueue): Adds an element with a specific priority.
Extract (Dequeue): Removes and returns the highest-priority element.

Under the Hood

Often implemented using a Binary Heap data structure, allowing fast $O(\log n)$ insertions and extractions.
